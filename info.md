# Simple-Calendars (Month, Week, Day & Event)

Suite of **4 minimalist Lovelace calendar cards** for Home Assistant with **multi-calendar** support:

- **Event Card** (`custom:simple-calendar-event-card`) — Quick tracker for previous, current (with progress bar), and upcoming events
- **Daily** (`custom:simple-calendar-day-card`)
- **Weekly** (`custom:simple-calendar-week-card`)
- **Monthly** (`custom:simple-calendar-month-card`)

Includes a complete visual editor, transparency controls, customizable colors and icons per entity, configurable past-event dimming (`dim_completed_events`, enabled by default across all 4 cards), automatic midnight rollover, and built-in translations in **10 languages** (English, Spanish, Simplified Chinese, German, Italian, French, Portuguese, Russian, Polish, and Dutch).
