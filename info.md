# Simple-Calendars (Month, Day & Focus Event)

Suite of **3 minimalist Lovelace calendar cards** for Home Assistant with **multi-calendar** support:

- **Focus Event Card** (`custom:simple-calendar-focus-event-card`) — Quick tracker for previous, current (with progress bar), and upcoming events
- **Daily** (`custom:simple-calendar-day-card`)
- **Monthly** (`custom:simple-calendar-month-card`) — Scalable `compact` / `normal` / `large` grid with pills or dots and Daily-style selected day summary

Includes a complete visual editor, transparency controls, customizable colors and icons per entity, configurable past-event dimming (`dim_completed_events`, enabled by default across all 3 cards), automatic midnight rollover, and built-in translations in **10 languages** (English, Spanish, Simplified Chinese, German, Italian, French, Portuguese, Russian, Polish, and Dutch).
